In this realm export, most custom client scopes have include.in.token.scope=false. That means they mainly add claims (audiences/roles) rather than appearing as a literal “scope string”. Your RBAC still works because roles are emitted via the roles client scope mappers.

Hardening suggestion

Scope mappings connect a client scope to one or more roles. In your export, one mapping is explicit:

Client Scope Mapped Roles Notes
offline_access offline_access Enables offline refresh token behavior when requested/allowed.
If you want “domain scopes” like orders-read to imply roles (e.g., order_read), add scope mappings for them as well. Otherwise, keep RBAC purely role-driven and treat scopes as “token shape”.
