Policy & Guardrails Architecture

Safety, compliance, and deterministic control for Agentic AI customer support workflows.

Why Guardrails Matter

In the Customer Support Triage + Action Agent, the model can reason and propose actions. However, business policy must be authoritative.

  • Prevent unauthorized refunds
  • Prevent policy-violating shipping upgrades
  • Enforce SLA and eligibility rules
  • Ensure auditability & compliance

State Machine with Policy Gate

Classify → Fetch → Policy Check → Clarify → Act → Respond
1️⃣ Intent Guardrails
  • Intent classification confidence threshold
  • Ambiguity detection
  • Disallowed categories filtering
  • Injection & jailbreak protection
2️⃣ Policy Engine
  • Replacement eligibility window
  • Shipping upgrade rules
  • Inventory availability validation
  • Refund thresholds
  • Fraud scoring integration
3️⃣ Execution Guardrails
  • Idempotency enforcement
  • Action approval workflow
  • Rate limiting
  • Transactional integrity
  • Audit logging
Example: Replacement Request
User: "My order #88421 was delivered to the wrong address.
       I want a replacement shipped overnight."

Policy Evaluation:
------------------------------------------
Delivery confirmed?        YES
Address mismatch verified? YES
Within replacement window? YES
Item in stock?             YES
Overnight allowed by SLA?  CHECK CUSTOMER TIER

Decision:
Replacement Approved
Shipping: Standard (Overnight not permitted for this tier)
Support Ticket: Opened

Core Guardrail Principles
Deterministic
Policy logic must never depend solely on LLM reasoning.
Auditable
Every decision produces a structured audit record.
Idempotent
Repeated calls cannot duplicate financial impact.
Least Privilege
Tool execution constrained by RBAC.
